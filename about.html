Two sides of the same counter.

Thomas grew up in his family's hardware business and then spent four years in sales software. Henry spent years building tools embedded with the operations and trading teams of a global trading firm. We started QuoteTrack because the trade has been running on systems that record what happened, and nothing that drives what happens next.

How we got here

We started by listening, not building.

Forty-plus conversations with sales directors, reps and IT directors across Ireland and the UK, and eight days stood behind counters and sat in branch offices. We didn't pitch. We asked people about their day.

The same thing came back every time. The quote goes out, and then it goes quiet. Not because anyone is bad at their job, but because there is nothing in the stack whose job it is to catch it. The ERP records it. The CRM, if there is one, gets used as a tick-box. And Friday afternoon disappears into admin.

The trade built this country brick by brick, and has been overlooked by software the whole time. Reps here are some of the sharpest operators you'll meet, selling through tenders, planning delays and supply shocks. They deserve tools built for how they actually work, and that is the only thing we're trying to do.

How we build

Three rules we hold ourselves to.

Keep it simple

A customer told us he'd looked at seven systems and every one was overkill. The test we hold ourselves to: could someone new on the counter understand it?

Push, not pull

If a rep has to log in to get value, they won't. The list comes to them, every morning, wherever they are.

Never surveillance

This is a tool for the rep, not a stick to beat them with. We design against that every time, and we always will.
