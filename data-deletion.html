Data deletion

How to ask us to delete personal data, and what happens when you do.

Who this page is for

QuoteTrack Limited provides a business service to trade distributors. We hold personal data in two ways: as a controller for our own website, enquiries and account users, and as a processor for data that a customer puts into QuoteTrack. The route to deletion depends on which applies to you.

If your data was put into QuoteTrack by a business you deal with

For example, if a distributor you buy from uses QuoteTrack and your name or contact details appear on a quote, then that distributor is the controller of that data. Please contact them first — they decide what is held and for how long. If they ask us to delete it, we will do so on their instructions. You can also email us at [privacy@quotetrack.io] and we will help identify the right customer and pass your request on.

If you are a QuoteTrack user or an account administrator

Email [privacy@quotetrack.io] from the email address on your QuoteTrack account, with the subject “Data deletion request”. Tell us whether you want:

  • your user account removed (name, email, login and activity data); or
  • your business’s data removed (for example, because you are closing your account). This normally needs an administrator of your business to make the request, and may be governed by your agreement with us.

We will acknowledge the request, ask for anything we need to confirm your identity and authority, and then carry it out.

If you contacted us through this website

Email [privacy@quotetrack.io] from the address you used, or tell us the name and company you used in the form, and we will remove the enquiry and its follow-up records.

What we delete and what we keep

We delete the personal data covered by your request, including account details and, where applicable, the records you ask us to remove. A few things may be kept for a limited period:

  • Legal and financial records we are required to keep (for example, invoices and tax records) — kept only for as long as the law requires.
  • Security and audit records — kept for a short period where needed to investigate misuse or meet legal obligations.
  • Backups — deleted data may remain in backups for up to [RETENTION PERIOD] before those backups are overwritten, and is not used for any other purpose while it remains there.

How long it takes

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days of confirming your identity and authority, and we will tell you when it is done. If a request is complex we may extend that period, and we will explain why.

Data received through Meta or WhatsApp

If a WhatsApp capture pilot is enabled in future, messages sent to the QuoteTrack bot would be processed through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform on the instructions of the participating business. To request deletion of data received that way, email [privacy@quotetrack.io] with the phone number used and, if you know it, the business you were messaging. We will delete what we hold, and the participating business can also action deletion of its own records.
