How it works

Connects to your ERP. Surfaces what matters.

1

Connects to your ERP

Pulls quote data directly from your ERP. Intact, Kerridge, Epicor and more. No disruption. Your data stays where it is.

2

Identifies at-risk quotes

Flags quotes by value, age, customer tier and behaviour automatically, across every branch.

3

Recommends next actions

Sales reps see a prioritised follow-up list. Managers see pipeline health. Everyone knows what to do next.
