How the work actually arrives

A quote request doesn't arrive in your ERP. It arrives everywhere else.

Email and drawings, a call, the trade counter, a voicenote from a van, a QS take-off. QuoteTrack takes all of it and gets a priced quote into your ERP. Then it picks the quote back up on the other side and chases it until there's an answer. The ERP stays exactly as it is. It's the database, and it keeps that job.
