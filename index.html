The gap

Everything comes in. Nothing gets chased.

A quote request reaches a merchant six different ways before lunch. Every one of them lands on the same rep, and every one of them has to be turned into a priced quote by hand.

  • WhatsApp
  • Phone call
  • Email & drawings
  • Trade counter
  • Voicenote
  • QS take-off

One rep

Reading drawings, checking stock, pricing lines, rebuilding a quote that half exists somewhere else.

2–3 hours to build one quote, by hand
The ERP records it.

Then the quote sits there. Nothing counts the days. Nothing raises a hand.

So the quote goes cold, and the rep becomes an order-taker.

The cost

7

days before a quote has gone cold

A rule of thumb we were given by a sales director in the trade: after about a week without contact, the quote is usually gone.

Ask a branch what it loses this way. Nobody can answer.

Not because nobody cares. Because nothing counts it. The quotes that were never chased do not appear in a report, do not show up in a month-end pack, and never get argued about in a meeting.

A cost you cannot see is a cost you never fix. That is the whole problem, and it is the first thing QuoteTrack changes.

How it works
