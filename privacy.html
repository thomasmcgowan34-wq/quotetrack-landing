Who we are

QuoteTrack Limited is a company registered in Ireland (company number [CRO NUMBER], VAT number [VAT NUMBER]) with its registered office at [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Ireland. In this policy, “QuoteTrack”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to that company.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at [privacy@quotetrack.io].

Our two roles

As a controller. We decide how we handle personal data about visitors to quotetrack.io, people who contact us, and users of the QuoteTrack app (for example, their account and login details).

As a processor. When a distributor uses QuoteTrack to manage its own quotes, it decides what data goes into the service and why. In that case the distributor is the controller and we process the data on its instructions, under a data processing agreement. If you are a customer of one of our customers and want to exercise your rights, please contact that customer first — we will assist them as required.

Personal data we handle

Website enquiries. If you request a demo or contact us through a form on this site, we receive the details you enter (such as your name, work email, company and message). The form is processed by our form provider, [FORMSPREE], on our behalf.

Account users. For people given a QuoteTrack login, we hold their name, work email address, job title, account status, the branches or teams they belong to, and security information such as login and session records.

Customer data processed as a processor. On behalf of a customer, the service stores and displays the business records that customer chooses to put in it — which may include quote records, customer and contact names, prices, line items, notes, activity history and attachments. We do not use this data for our own purposes.

Support and feedback. If you send us a support request or product feedback, we keep the content so we can respond and improve the service.

Security and technical records. Like most online services we keep limited technical records (for example, timestamps, IP-derived request information and error logs) needed to run and secure the service.

We do not intend to collect special-category data (for example, health information) through QuoteTrack. Please do not submit it.

Why we process personal data

  • To provide the service — setting up accounts, displaying quotes, sending operational email such as password resets and daily digests (performance of a contract).
  • To run and secure it — access control, security monitoring, backups, troubleshooting and preventing misuse (our legitimate interests, and those of our customers).
  • To respond to you — handling demo requests, support and feedback (our legitimate interests).
  • To meet legal obligations — for example, tax and accounting records.
  • With consent — where we ask for it, for example for optional product updates. You can withdraw consent at any time.

How we protect data

We use access controls and least-privilege accounts, row-level security in the database, encryption in transit, backups and monitored deployments. No online service can promise perfect security, but we take these measures seriously and review them as the service grows.

Your rights

Under data protection law you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • ask us to delete data;
  • ask us to restrict or stop processing in some circumstances;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests; and
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To make a request, email [privacy@quotetrack.io]. If your data was put into QuoteTrack by one of our customers, we will usually route your request to that customer, who is the controller. If you are not happy with how we have handled your request, you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28, Ireland — www.dataprotection.ie).

Automated decisions

We do not use your personal data to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Children

QuoteTrack is a business service. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
